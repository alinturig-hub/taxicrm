# TaxiCRM Project Principles

Version: 1.0

## Mission

TaxiCRM helps taxi companies understand, optimise and grow their business through data, analytics and artificial intelligence.

---

## Principle 1

TaxiCRM is an Operations Intelligence Platform.

It is not a dispatch system.

---

## Principle 2

Operational systems are the source of operational data.

TaxiCRM is the source of business intelligence.

---

## Principle 3

Every piece of data must create value.

Data should support:

- Analytics
- KPIs
- AI
- Business decisions

---

## Principle 4

Every screen must answer a business question.

If it doesn't help management make a better decision, it doesn't belong in TaxiCRM.

---

## Principle 5

AI must explain:

- What happened
- Why it happened
- What is likely to happen next
- What action should be taken

---

## Principle 6

Everything should be measurable.

Every business process should generate metrics.

---

## Principle 7

Business first.

Technology exists to serve the business.

---

## Principle 8

Historical data is an asset.

Never lose information that can improve future analysis.

---

## Principle 9

Design for growth.

Every module should scale to millions of bookings.

---

## Principle 10

The goal of TaxiCRM is to help taxi company owners make better decisions every day.
