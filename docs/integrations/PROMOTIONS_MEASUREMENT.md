# Promotions measurement and loyalty policy

Pareto is a descriptive ranking by observed completed-trip contribution, not an
automatic discount entitlement. Determine the real cumulative distribution; do
not assume exactly 20% of customers generate 80%. Active high-contribution customers
need relevant benefits rather than blanket discounts. First candidate: declining
frequency among historically valuable customers, with operator review.

Promotion cohorts must be frozen before contact and comparable across the same
London business observation window. No data-driven threshold, discount percentage
or automatic AI grant is activated by this document. Retention, second-trip,
reactivation and off-peak offers require approved commercial rules and budget.

UTM helpers use campaign/template references only, never customer identity, phone,
email or bearer credentials. Destination origins must be validated against approved
booking hosts by the future runtime caller. UTMs identify visits, not verified
customers or proof of attribution. Identity-safe campaign/benefit/customer/booking
mapping and phone bookings require authenticated downstream events.

Measurement helper accepts already-deduplicated canonical booking outcomes. Counts
COMPLETED only; computes Price minus Cost in GBP pence with signed differences.
If Price already includes the discount, never subtract it again. Spend means costs
not already reflected in booking margin, including explicit additional funding and
channel costs. Missing amounts suppress incremental financial estimates. Zero spend
has undefined ROI. Control-adjusted contribution is a per-customer comparison, not
net profit or causal proof. Real randomization, contamination checks, confidence
intervals and post-campaign retention measurement remain pending.

Persisted click events, audience assignment, attribution windows, provider delivery
receipts and reporting UI require further schema/API work. This is pure calculation
and tracking-link infrastructure; no live tracking or campaign execution is enabled.
