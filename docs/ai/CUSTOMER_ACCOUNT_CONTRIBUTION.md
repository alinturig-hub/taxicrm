# Customer observations and contribution review

## Scope

Local development on `codex/taxicrm`, verified base `3212ddfff21ed6a29e46f3a39186a0a1c28fe538`. No live reads, migrations, backfill, merge, push, deploy or model calls are required. No schema changes.

Business Analytics links to `/dashboard/analytics/contribution`. The report is on demand, not polling, and requires existing booking, report, revenue and customer read permissions on both page and API. All selected filters are printed with the returned snapshot. Failed runs clear the previous report instead of displaying it under new filters.

## Attribution and finance

Only COMPLETED bookings whose pickup timestamps fall in the selected London period contribute. Existing accountId takes priority over normalCustomerId. Account identity is the exact provider + accountId; non-account contacts use provider + existing normalCustomerId. No names, telephone numbers, email addresses or fuzzy matches create identities. Provider namespaces remain separate. A linked customer on an account booking is not credited with that account's receipts. Non-account contacts are not asserted to be the verified payer or a unique passenger. Missing identities are counted and excluded from Pareto; full denominator reconciliation remains visible. Account id presence is a recorded-source association, not a newly verified billing relationship.

Separate account/contact Pareto views cover completed frequency, gross receipts and margin before expenses. Monetary aggregation rounds each source amount to integer pence in PostgreSQL; JavaScript rejects unsafe integers. Source values are not modified. Financial totals are not silently substituted with fare, Cost or MAX(Price,Cost). Negative amounts are retained. A financial Pareto is unavailable when that group's required amounts are incomplete, any entity has a negative contribution, or its denominator is nonpositive. Known negative entity totals remain visible. No claims about exact 80/20 structure, net profit, discount funding or driver fault.

The smallest number of contributors reaching 80% and the actual share generated by the top ceil(20% of contributors) are reported separately. All returned contributor groups enter denominators; top 20 rows are displayed. Read-only repeatable-read transaction, 12s statement timeout, 2s lock timeout, 8MB work_mem, no parallel query workers, 20s transaction cap, maximum 31 London dates and 50,000 grouped identities; overflow refuses the report rather than truncating percentages. At most four report calculations per process; authenticated identity cooldown applies. Cluster-wide rate limits remain deployment work.

## Observed customer activity

Existing profile output now includes `observations`, displayed in the customer profile overview. This computes completed-pickup recency, median positive elapsed interval, recent/previous trailing 30 elapsed-day counts, and frequent London weekday/hour slots. Missing/invalid pickup times, future completed pickups and repeated provider/reference pairs are counted and excluded. Booking time is not substituted for an unknown pickup. Same-time distinct trips remain separate observations; zero intervals do not enter the median. Four positive intervals are the descriptive-history minimum, not a calibration guarantee. Observation signals are not personal intent, churn classification, or predicted booking probabilities. Existing shared-contact warnings still apply.

Corrected the stale profile `valueBasis` label from MAX_COST_PRICE to COMPLETED_PRICE: its calculation already uses Price through bookingValue. No financial values or existing tags were rewritten.
