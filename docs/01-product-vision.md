# TaxiCRM Product Vision

Version: 1.0

---

# Mission

TaxiCRM is an AI-powered Operations Intelligence Platform for taxi companies.

Its purpose is to help operators understand, optimise and grow their business using data, analytics and artificial intelligence.

TaxiCRM complements dispatch software such as Autocab.

It does not replace it.

---

# Vision

Taxi company owners should be able to understand the health of their business in less than five minutes.

TaxiCRM should answer questions such as:

- Where does revenue come from?
- Where are we losing money?
- Which customers create the most value?
- Which drivers perform best?
- Which zones are growing?
- Why are bookings rejected?
- What should we improve next?

---

# Core Pillars

1. Collect operational data.

2. Transform data into analytics.

3. Discover business patterns.

4. Generate AI insights.

5. Support better decisions.

---

# What TaxiCRM IS

- Operations Intelligence Platform
- Business Analytics Platform
- AI Decision Support Platform
- Business Performance Platform

---

# What TaxiCRM IS NOT

- Dispatch System
- Booking System
- Fleet Dispatch Console
- Replacement for Autocab

---

# Long-Term Goal

Build the operating system that every taxi company owner uses to understand, optimise and grow their business.
